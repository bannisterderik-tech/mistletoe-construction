Roof Replacement

Do I need to be home for a roof replacement?

Short answer: no. A reroof is almost entirely outside work, and most Douglas County homeowners go about their day while our crew installs. Here's exactly what to expect on install day, how to prep your home and pets, and the few things we do need from you.

⚡ Quick answer

No — you usually don't need to be home. A roof replacement is almost entirely exterior work, so most homeowners go to work or run errands while we install. We just need clear access to the roof and driveway, an outdoor power outlet, and a phone number to reach you. Many people like to be home for the kickoff and the final walkthrough, but that's your choice. It's loud, so plan around pets and anyone working from home. Most Umpqua Valley homes take one to three days start to finish.

It's one of the most common questions we hear once someone books a reroof, and it's a good one — nobody wants to burn a vacation day sitting in the driveway. The reassuring truth is that a roof replacement barely touches the inside of your home. Everything that matters happens over your head, outside. Here's the full picture so you can plan your day with confidence.

What we actually need from you

Not much, and none of it requires you to stand around watching:

  • Roof and driveway access. We stage material and a dumpster near the house, so we need the driveway clear and a path to the roof.
  • An outdoor power outlet. For compressors and tools. If you don't have one accessible, just let us know ahead of time.
  • A phone number. So we can reach you with any questions — for instance, if we open the deck and find rot to discuss, which happens on our older, damp Umpqua Valley homes.
  • A heads-up on anything unusual. Septic lines, a well, delicate landscaping, a gate code — tell us in advance and we'll work around it.

That's genuinely it. You can be at work in Roseburg or out of town entirely, and the job runs the same.

Do we come inside?

Rarely. During the estimate we may ask to check the attic to look at the deck and ventilation — see our attic ventilation guide for why that matters here. But on install day the crew works entirely from outside. You do not need to leave the house unlocked or give interior access while you're away, unless we've specifically agreed on attic or ceiling work beforehand. For the full scope of what a reroof involves, our what a roof replacement includes guide walks every layer.

How to prep your home

A little prep makes the day smoother:

  • Move your cars. Park on the street or well away from the house so there's room for the dumpster and materials, and nothing sits under falling debris.
  • Protect what's fragile inside. Tearing off and nailing a roof vibrates the house. Take down wall art and shelved breakables directly under the roof, especially in older homes.
  • Clear the perimeter. Move patio furniture, grills, and potted plants away from the walls. We tarp and protect landscaping, but giving us room helps.
  • Plan for pets and kids. More on that next — it's the part people underestimate.

Pets and the noise

This is the real reason many Douglas County homeowners choose to be elsewhere: a reroof is loud. It's a full day of hammering and footsteps overhead. Dogs and cats find it genuinely stressful, so plenty of our customers board pets or leave them with a friend for install day. If anyone in the house works from home or naps young kids, plan to be somewhere quieter.

How long it takes

Most single-family homes in the Umpqua Valley take one to three days once materials arrive. Steep pitches, cut-up rooflines, or hidden deck repairs can add a day. Because we roof through Oregon's wet season, we watch weather windows closely and will confirm your date as it approaches — our new-roof timeline guide and winter roofing guide explain how we schedule around the rain.

The end of the job

When we finish, we run a magnetic sweep across the yard and driveway to collect stray nails, haul away every scrap of debris, and — if you're available — do a final walkthrough with photos of the finished roof and your warranty paperwork. If you can't be there, we document everything and follow up. You'll be able to use your home normally that same evening. We're not finished until you'd point a neighbor our way.

Quick answers

Can the whole job happen while I'm at work?

Yes, for the exterior install. Just leave us driveway access, an outdoor outlet, and a phone number. Many customers only come home for the final walkthrough.

Will my house be secure with a crew there?

The work is outside and you don't need to leave anything unlocked. We treat your property like our own and clean up completely at the end.

What if it rains mid-job?

We never leave a roof open to weather — we dry-in each area and plan around forecast windows. Our winter roofing guide explains how.
