New roof cost in Douglas County, Oregon.

Quick answer

In 2026, most new asphalt-shingle roofs in Douglas County run roughly $10,000–$25,000 with a full tear-off — about $475–$775 per square of architectural shingles, depending on pitch. Metal runs about 1.9× the asphalt figure. The county requires a full tear-off (no overlays) and generally a permit over 100 sq ft, both of which are built into local pricing. Your roof's size, steepness, and condition set where you land. Every number here is an estimate — a written on-site estimate is free.

2026 Douglas County price ranges

  • Architectural asphalt: most homes land $10,000–$25,000, full tear-off included. Small simple roofs dip under; big, steep, complex ones go over.
  • Per square (100 sq ft): roughly $475–$775 installed for architectural asphalt — tear-off, underlayment, flashing, and shingles. Low end is walkable pitch; high end is steep and harness-required.
  • Metal: about 1.9× asphalt for standing-seam — see the Oregon metal roof cost guide for the payback math.
  • Repairs, not replacement: if you're not sure you need a whole roof, the Oregon roof repair cost guide covers targeted fixes from about $350.

A rough way to sanity-check a bid: measure your home's footprint, add for overhangs and pitch to estimate squares (most homes are 18–30), and multiply by the per-square range. If a bid lands far under that, someone's cutting underlayment, flashing, the permit, or insurance.

Local rules that shape your price

Two Douglas County requirements are baked into every honest local bid:

  • No overlays. The county does not allow new shingles over old, so every reroof is a full tear-off to the deck. That costs more than a second layer would — and it's genuinely better, because the deck gets inspected and repaired instead of buried.
  • Permits. Reroofs over 100 square feet generally require a permit, and kick-out flashing at sidewalls is required. We pull the permit as part of the job. Our Douglas County permit guide has the specifics.
