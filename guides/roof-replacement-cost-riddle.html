What Riddle roofs are up against

Riddle sits low in the Cow Creek valley, ringed by timbered hills. Down on the valley floor, shaded and tree-lined roof planes stay damp and grow moss, and fir and oak litter clogs gutters and valleys every fall. Up on the hillsides, homes catch more wind along their ridgelines, which lifts shingle edges and ridge caps over time. And like everywhere on the wet side, the wet-winter, hot-summer swing bakes then soaks asphalt until the granules wash away. Architectural shingles with algae-resistant granules — the line we install most — hold up to that cycle far better than old 3-tab.

Permits and the no-overlay rule

Riddle falls under Douglas County building rules: reroofs over 100 square feet generally require a permit, and overlays aren't allowed, so every replacement is a full tear-off to the deck.
