1. Scope of Work.

Contractor agrees to furnish labor, materials, equipment, supervision, and services necessary to complete the work described in the attached Proposal/Estimate. Any work not specifically listed in the Proposal is excluded.

2. Contract Documents.

The Contract consists of this Master Construction Agreement, the attached Proposal/Estimate, approved Change Orders, manufacturer specifications (when applicable), and warranty documents.

3. Contract Price.

Owner agrees to pay Contractor the amount shown on the attached Proposal plus any approved Change Orders. Applicable taxes, permit fees, and unforeseen conditions not included within the Proposal shall be billed separately unless specifically included.

4. Payment Terms.

Residential Projects: 50% deposit due upon acceptance; remaining balance due according to the attached Proposal or immediately upon substantial completion unless otherwise stated. The deposit is used for project scheduling, labor, mobilization, ordering materials, and reserving installation dates. Commercial Projects: unless otherwise stated, Net 30 payment terms; progress billing may be utilized; invoices not paid within thirty (30) days shall be considered past due.

5. Late Payments.

Past due balances shall accrue interest at the lesser of 1.5% per month or the maximum rate permitted by Oregon law. Owner agrees to pay all reasonable collection costs, attorney fees, court costs, and lien filing expenses if collection becomes necessary.

6. Change Orders.

No additional work shall be performed without written approval from Owner except when emergency conditions threaten life or property. Approved Change Orders become part of this Agreement.

7. Concealed Conditions.

Contract price does not include repairs resulting from concealed conditions including but not limited to rotten wood, structural deficiencies, mold, water damage, dry rot, insect or termite damage, asbestos, lead paint, hidden electrical or plumbing deficiencies, foundation movement, and code violations not visible during estimating. Discovery of concealed conditions shall constitute additional work.

8. Sheathing and Structural Repairs.

Roof decking, wall sheathing, framing, rafters, trusses, fascia, soffit, blocking, or structural members requiring replacement are excluded unless specifically listed. Owner shall approve additional repairs before work proceeds whenever reasonably possible.

9. Building Code Requirements.

If code-required repairs become necessary after work begins, Contractor shall notify Owner. Additional code-required work shall be billed separately unless specifically included.

10. Material Availability.

Material shortages, discontinued products, shipping delays, manufacturer delays, or supply chain interruptions may delay completion. Contractor may substitute substantially equivalent materials with Owner approval when necessary.

11. Weather Delays.

Contractor shall not be responsible for delays caused by rain, snow, ice, high winds, excessive heat, smoke, wildfire, acts of God, or government restrictions. Completion dates shall automatically extend for such delays.

12. Owner Responsibilities.

Owner agrees to provide clear jobsite access, maintain pets away from work areas, remove fragile personal property, provide water and electricity when reasonably necessary, and obtain HOA approvals unless otherwise agreed.

13. Permits.

Unless specifically stated otherwise, Contractor shall obtain required permits. Permit fees are included only when listed in the Proposal.

14. Subcontractors.

Contractor may utilize qualified subcontractors while remaining responsible for completion of the contracted work.

15. Property Protection.

Contractor shall exercise reasonable care to protect the property. Owner understands construction activities may result in dust, noise, vibration, lawn damage, landscape disturbance, nail debris, tire marks, and minor incidental damage typical of construction.

16. Cleanup.

Contractor shall remove construction debris generated by Contractor and perform a general cleanup upon completion. Deep cleaning or professional housekeeping is excluded unless specifically included.

17. Customer-Supplied Materials.

Contractor provides no warranty on customer-supplied materials. Labor associated with defective customer materials remains billable.

18. Roofing Provisions.

Owner understands roofing may expose concealed defects. Roof decking replacement is not included unless specifically stated. Roof color variations between production lots are considered normal. Roof completion dates remain weather dependent.

19. Remodeling Provisions.

Hidden plumbing, electrical, framing, insulation, drywall, or structural deficiencies discovered during demolition constitute additional work.

20. Insurance Claims.

Contractor is not responsible for insurance company coverage decisions. Owner remains responsible for all contract amounts regardless of insurance reimbursement.

21. Warranties.

Contractor warrants workmanship for one (1) year from substantial completion unless otherwise stated. Warranty excludes acts of God, windstorms, hail, earthquakes, fire, abuse, neglect, lack of maintenance, foot traffic, other contractors, alterations by others, normal wear, and manufacturer defects. Manufacturer warranties remain solely the responsibility of the manufacturer.

22. Warranty Claims.

Owner shall notify Contractor in writing within a reasonable time after discovering any alleged defect. Contractor shall be given a reasonable opportunity to inspect and correct covered workmanship issues before Owner hires others.

23. Suspension of Work.

Contractor may suspend work for non-payment, unsafe conditions, lack of access, Owner interference, or permit issues beyond Contractor's control. Time extensions shall apply accordingly.

24. Termination.

Contractor may terminate this Agreement for material breach by Owner, including failure to make required payments. Owner shall pay for all completed work, ordered materials, restocking fees, mobilization costs, and reasonable overhead incurred through the date of termination.

25. Mechanic's Lien Rights.

Contractor reserves all rights available under Oregon law to file and enforce construction liens for unpaid labor, materials, services, and authorized extras.

26. Dispute Resolution.

The parties agree to attempt good-faith negotiation before commencing litigation. If mutually agreed, disputes may be submitted to mediation before filing suit. Nothing in this Agreement waives any statutory rights available under Oregon law.

27. Attorney Fees.

In any action arising from this Agreement, the prevailing party shall be entitled to recover reasonable attorney fees, expert witness fees, court costs, and expenses to the extent permitted by Oregon law.

28. Force Majeure.

Neither party shall be liable for delays caused by events beyond reasonable control including natural disasters, labor shortages, strikes, pandemics, governmental actions, supplier failures, transportation interruptions, or utility failures.

29. Photographs.

Contractor may photograph the project before, during, and after construction for documentation, warranty administration, quality control, and insurance purposes. Photographs used for marketing will not identify the Owner without permission unless otherwise permitted by law.

30. Entire Agreement.

This Agreement, together with the attached Proposal and approved Change Orders, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties. No oral statements modify this Agreement.

31. Severability.

If any provision is determined unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

32. Electronic Signatures.

Electronic signatures shall have the same force and effect as original signatures.

33. Governing Law.

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Oregon.

34. Oregon Consumer Notice.

For residential projects subject to Oregon law, Contractor will provide all notices required by the Oregon Construction Contractors Board (CCB), including applicable consumer protection information and lien-related notices. The Owner acknowledges receipt of any required notices provided with this Agreement or before work begins.
