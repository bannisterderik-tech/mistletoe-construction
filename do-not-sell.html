Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information

Short version: we don't sell your data — never have, never will. This page lets you make it official, or request access, correction, or deletion.

Effective date: July 24, 2026

Mistletoe Construction LLC does not sell personal information, and we do not "share" it for cross-context behavioral advertising as those terms are defined by the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA) or the Oregon Consumer Privacy Act. We're a family-owned roofing company in Riddle, Oregon — your information goes toward fixing your roof, not into a data broker's spreadsheet.

Even though we don't sell or share data, you can still submit a formal request below and we will honor it. You can also request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. We will confirm receipt and respond within 45 days, as required by the CCPA. For details on what we collect and why, see our Privacy Policy.
