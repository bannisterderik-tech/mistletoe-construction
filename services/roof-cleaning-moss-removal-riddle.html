Local Knowledge

Riddle's valley keeps roofs wet — we know, we live here.

Riddle sits in a tight pocket of the South Umpqua Valley at I-5 exit 103, tucked below Nickel Mountain with the river winding past the edge of town. That geography is lovely and it's murder on roofs: cool valley air pools overnight, morning fog lingers, and shaded lots under the surrounding timber stay damp long after the rest of the county has dried out. Moss doesn't need much more encouragement than that.

As a small former mining and timber town, Riddle has a lot of older ranch and manufactured homes with low-slope roofs — exactly the surfaces where moss establishes fastest and holds water longest. We've worked more roofs within a few miles of our own front door than we can count, so we know which streets stay shaded and which roofs are due.

  • Valley fog & shade — cool air pooling below Nickel Mountain keeps roofs damp well past sunrise
  • Low-slope roofs — common on Riddle's older ranch and manufactured homes, where water and moss linger
  • Riverside moisture — homes near the South Umpqua carry higher humidity that feeds moss
  • Timber-town debris — surrounding conifers drop needles that hold moisture and clog gutters
  • Fast local response — we're minutes away, so small moss problems get handled before they become leaks

Our Method

Hand removal. Treatment. Prevention. Never pressure washing.

A pressure washer makes a mossy Riddle roof look great for one summer — while blasting off the granules your shingles need to survive the next twenty winters. We don't own one for roof work, on purpose.

01

Inspect & photograph

Since we're right here in Riddle, we can often swing by the same week to map the moss, check for damage underneath, and photograph everything before quoting.

02

Gentle hand removal

Soft brushes and hand tools lift the moss mat without tearing granules or lifting shingles — slower than a pressure washer, and far kinder to your roof.

03

Treat what remains

A moss treatment kills the roots and spores the brushes can't reach, so regrowth starts from zero, not from a head start.

04

Prevent regrowth

Zinc strips at the ridge fight regrowth every time it rains — and being local, we can fold your Riddle roof into seasonal checks so it never gets bad again. Our prevention guide has the details.
