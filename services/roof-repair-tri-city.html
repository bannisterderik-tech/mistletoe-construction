Tri City's setting — South Umpqua, close to home

Tri City is an unincorporated community in southern Douglas County, sitting right along I-5 and the old Highway 99 between Myrtle Creek to the north and Canyonville to the south, in the South Umpqua river valley. It reads as a close neighbor of Myrtle Creek — a mix of established homes, newer houses, and rural properties on the surrounding slopes — and it is right in the heart of our home territory. Our shop in Riddle is only a few freeway exits away, which makes Tri City one of the quickest calls we run.

The climate here is the South Umpqua valley pattern that we know intimately from working Riddle, Canyonville, and Myrtle Creek every week: wet, soaking winters from fall into spring, then a hot, dry summer that bakes the sun-facing slopes. That wet-then-scorched cycle ages asphalt, and the oak-and-fir shade on north-facing planes keeps moss and clogged gutters a year-round concern.

The repairs we do most in Tri City

  • Active leaks. Traced from the attic and the roof, not guessed at from the driveway — and we can get out fast when water is coming in.
  • Flashing failures. Chimneys, sidewalls, skylights, and vents are where most Tri City leaks actually start.
  • Moss on shaded planes. The South Umpqua shade grows moss that lifts shingle edges and traps water. We clear, repair, and treat.
  • Wind and storm damage. Winter storms peel tabs on exposed slopes; we replace and re-seal.
  • Clogged gutters and eave rot. Oak leaves and fir needles overflow gutters and rot fascia; we repair and can add guards.

How a Tri City roof repair works

We find the real source of the water before quoting. We inspect from the attic and the roof, photograph what we find, and give you a clear written price up front. When the repair is done, you get before-and-after photos, useful for your records and for insurance.

Our repairs are built to shed water and stay dry through a wet South Umpqua winter: correctly lapped flashing, sealed penetrations, and moss treatment on the planes that grow it fastest. Where clogged gutters are feeding the leak, we handle the drainage as part of the fix. And because Tri City is only about 15 minutes from our Riddle shop, an active leak gets one of the quickest responses we offer anywhere in the county.
