Our Story

Meet the family behind Mistletoe Construction.

We're not a call center with a truck wrap. We're a family in Riddle, roofing the same Douglas County towns we live in, shop in, and raise our kids in. Here's who you're actually calling.

⚡ Instant Roof Estimate Call (541) 670-5005

By Mistletoe Construction Oregon CCB #255729 Published August 1, 2026 6 min read

⚡ The short version

Mistletoe Construction is a family-owned roofing and construction company based in Riddle, Oregon, serving all of Douglas County. We're licensed and insured under Oregon CCB #255729, we're an Owens Corning contractor, and the person who answers the phone is the same person you'll see on your roof. That's the whole model: the roofer next door, held to a next-door standard.

There's a certain kind of roofing company that blows into a town after a big storm, works fast, and is three counties away by the time the first callback comes in. We're the opposite of that, on purpose. Mistletoe Construction is family-owned and rooted right here in Riddle — 595 E Third Street, if you want to know where the trucks sleep — and we've built the whole company around a simple idea: we have to see these customers again. At the grocery store. At the game. At church. In a county this size, your reputation isn't a marketing budget, it's your actual life.

Why "Mistletoe"?

People ask about the name. It's an Oregon thing. Anybody who's driven the oak country around the Umpqua Valley in winter has seen those green bundles up in the bare branches — mistletoe, holding on through the wettest, greyest stretch of the year when everything else has gone dormant. That felt about right for a company that does its hardest work in the season nobody else wants to be on a roof. Green when it's grey. Steady when the weather isn't.

A family operation, not a franchise

Family-owned gets printed on a lot of trucks that don't mean it. For us it's the operating structure, not a slogan. There's no regional manager, no rotating cast of subcontractors we've never met, no script. When you call (541) 670-5005, you reach us — and the crew that shows up to look at your roof is the crew that does the work and the crew that comes back if something ever isn't right. That continuity is the thing you can't fake. It's why we're comfortable telling a homeowner their roof has seven good years left instead of selling them a replacement they don't need: we'd rather have the neighbor than the invoice.

What we actually stand for

Every company says they're honest. Here's what we mean by it in practice, on a roof, on a Tuesday:

We tell you the real answer. Our free inspection ends in one of three words — maintain, repair, or replace — backed by photos of what we actually found. If a repair is the right call, we'll say so even though a replacement pays us more. That's not charity; it's how a local business survives past its first few years.

We do it once, right. We're an Owens Corning contractor because we'd rather install materials built for western Oregon's soak-then-bake climate than shave a few dollars off the bid with something that won't see twenty winters. Douglas County doesn't allow shingle overlays, and honestly we wouldn't do them anyway — a proper tear-off lets us see and fix the decking underneath. If you want to know what a real reroof includes, our inspection checklist and guides lay it all out with nothing hidden.

We clean up like our mother's watching. Because she might be. A jobsite left full of nails and shingle scraps tells you everything about how a company thinks about your home. We treat the walkthrough at the end as seriously as the roof itself.

More than roofs

Roofing is the heart of what we do, but "Construction" is in the name for a reason. We're a general contractor, so a lot of our Douglas County neighbors know us for siding, gutter guards, decks, fences, and the kind of general work that keeps an Oregon home tight against the weather. It means we can look at a house as a whole system — the way water actually moves across a roof, down the gutters, past the siding, and away from the foundation — instead of just the one square we were called about. You can see the full list on our services page.

Rooted in Douglas County

We roof homes from Roseburg down through Myrtle Creek and Canyonville, out to Winston and Camas Valley, up to Sutherlin and Oakland, and all the small places in between. We know these roofs because we live under the same sky — the same 130 rainy days, the same moss on the north slopes, the same Coast Range wind, the same dry summer that bakes it all. A crew that learned roofing in Phoenix or Dallas is genuinely guessing about this climate. We're not. Every guide on our resources page came out of questions real neighbors asked us, and we answer them free whether or not anyone ever hires us.

Being the roofer next door

Here's the promise, and it's a short one: we'll treat your home the way we'd want ours treated, we'll tell you the truth about what it needs, and we'll still be here — same name, same number, same family — when you need us again. That's the entire value proposition. No gimmicks, just people you can find.

You can read more about our licensing, our service area, and how we work over on our About page, or see recent jobs on Our Work.
