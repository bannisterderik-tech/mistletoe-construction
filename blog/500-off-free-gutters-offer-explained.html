$500 off + free gutters: how our offer works.

When a company advertises "free" anything, a smart homeowner squints. Good. Let's do the opposite of fine print — here's exactly how our current roofing offer works, who qualifies, and what to expect, in plain language from a family crew in Riddle.

⚡ The offer in one line

Every new roof we install right now comes with $500 off. And on qualifying roofs — jobs of $20,000 or more — you also get free whole-home gutters. That's it. No coupon to clip, no gimmick, no "call in the next ten minutes." Mention it when we come out and it's applied to your written estimate.

We put this offer together because two things are true at once in the Umpqua Valley: a new roof is a real expense, and a roof without good gutters doesn't do its whole job. So instead of a vague "discounts available," here's the actual deal, spelled out the way we'd want it explained to us.

The $500 off — the simple part

Every new roof replacement we install includes $500 off the price. It applies to all new roofs, not a select few, and it's not tied to a particular shingle line or a mystery "list price" we quietly inflated first. When we hand you a written estimate, the $500 is already reflected in the number. There's nothing to redeem and nothing to mail in — you just tell us you saw the offer.

The free whole-home gutters — the part with a threshold

The free gutters kick in on qualifying roofs of $20,000 or more. On a job that size, we install new gutters around the whole home at no additional charge. There's a plain reason for the threshold: whole-home gutters are a real material-and-labor cost, and it only pencils out for us to include them on a full-size roof replacement. On smaller jobs the $500 still applies — the free gutters are the bonus that comes with a larger reroof.

Why gutters at all? Because in a climate that throws 30-plus inches of rain at your house, gutters are what carry all that water away from your foundation, siding, and landscaping. A brand-new roof feeding into old, sagging, or leaking gutters is a half-finished job. Pairing the two means the water that sheets off your fresh roof actually leaves the building the way it should. If you want the deeper reasoning, our gutters for Oregon rain guide covers sizing and why fir needles make good gutters non-negotiable here.

Who qualifies

This is refreshingly short. If you own a home anywhere in our service area — Roseburg, Riddle, Winston, Myrtle Creek, Sutherlin, Canyonville and the rest of Douglas County — and you're getting a new roof from us, the $500 off applies. If that new roof is a qualifying job of $20,000 or more, the free whole-home gutters come with it. That's the whole gate. There's no income test, no "new customers only" catch, and no requirement to bundle other services.

The honest fine print

We'd rather you hear the caveats from us than feel surprised later, so here they are, straight:

It applies to new roof replacements. The offer is built around full reroofs, which is where the gutters and the savings make sense. A small repair is a different kind of job — though if you're weighing the two, our repair or replace guide helps you decide honestly.

The gutter benefit is tied to the $20k qualifying threshold. That number reflects a normal full-home roof replacement in our area; it isn't a hoop we invented to make the offer look bigger than it is. Our new roof cost in Douglas County page shows where local jobs typically land so you know roughly where you sit.

It's a current offer. Promotions don't run forever. If it matters to your timing, the safe move is to get your free estimate now and lock the terms in writing rather than assume it'll still be running next season.

We won't fake the base price. The oldest trick in home improvement is marking a price up so a "discount" costs you nothing to give. We don't do that. You're welcome to get other bids and compare — in fact, our how to read a roofing estimate guide is built to help you do exactly that, with us included.

Why we can offer this and still do it right

We're a family-owned company based in Riddle — Oregon CCB #255729, an Owens Corning contractor — not a storm-chasing outfit passing through. Our margins come from doing efficient, high-quality work across a service area we actually live in, not from squeezing every job. Bundling the savings and the gutters into full reroofs is simply a good deal we can stand behind, because a well-roofed, well-guttered house is one we're proud to have our name on. That's also why the free inspection and the estimate are genuinely free and pressure-free: if your roof isn't ready to replace, we'll tell you, offer or no offer.

The recap

$500 off every new roof. Free whole-home gutters on qualifying roofs of $20,000 or more. Anywhere in Douglas County. No coupon, no gimmick, applied right on your written estimate. It's a current offer, so don't sit on it.

How to claim it

Easiest first step: run our instant estimate for a fast ballpark, then book the free on-roof inspection so we can put a real written price — offer already applied — in your hands. Or just call or text us and say you saw the deal. Either way, you'll get a straight number from neighbors, with the $500 (and, if you qualify, the gutters) built right in.
