Our Story · From the Blog

Why we started Mistletoe Construction.

We didn't set out to build the biggest roofing company in Oregon. We set out to be the one our own neighbors in Douglas County could trust — the kind that tells you the truth about your roof and lets you sleep better when it rains.

By Mistletoe Construction Oregon CCB #255729 Published June 5, 2026 6 min read

The short version

We're a family-owned roofing and construction company based in Riddle, Oregon. We started Mistletoe because we were tired of watching neighbors get talked into roofs they didn't need, or into cheap ones that failed in our climate. Our whole promise fits in one line: do honest work so people sleep better when it rains. That's it. That's the company.

If you've lived in the Umpqua Valley for a winter, you already understand the feeling that started this company. It's late, it's dark, and the rain isn't falling so much as leaning on the house. You're lying in bed listening to it, and somewhere in the back of your mind is one question: is the roof going to hold? For a lot of our neighbors, the honest answer was "I'm not sure" — and that uncertainty is exactly what we built Mistletoe Construction to fix.

A family, a small town, and a lot of bad roofs

We're a family business, and we're proud that it's a small-town one. We're based in Riddle — 595 E Third Street, if you want to picture it — a town where you run into your customers at the grocery store and the post office. That changes how you do business. When the person whose roof you just finished is going to be standing behind you in line at Ray's next week, you don't cut corners. You can't. Your reputation isn't a review score online; it's your actual name, in your actual town.

For years, in the trades around here, we kept seeing the same two things happen to good people. Either a slick out-of-town outfit would roll through after a windstorm, scare a homeowner into a full replacement they didn't need, take the deposit, and vanish. Or a homeowner trying to save money would hire the cheapest bid, get a roof built for a dry climate instead of ours, and watch it fail years early under the moss and rain. Both stories ended the same way: a family out real money and still lying awake listening to the rain.

We figured a small, local, honest company could do better than that. So we started one.

Where the name comes from

People ask about the name. Mistletoe is one of those things that's woven into life out here — you see it up in the oaks all over Douglas County, especially in winter when the leaves are gone and it stands out green against the gray. It's a small, hardy, distinctly local thing that hangs on through the wet season. That felt about right for a family roofing company from Riddle: rooted here, built for this weather, still standing when things get harsh. We liked that the name couldn't belong to a national chain. It belongs to this valley.

What "sleep better when it rains" actually means

That line isn't marketing to us — it's the job, stated plainly. A roof has exactly one purpose, and it's not to look good in a photo. It's to stand between your family and the weather so completely that you stop thinking about it. When we say we want you to sleep better when it rains, we mean we want the roof to disappear from your worries entirely. No bucket in the hallway. No brown stain creeping across the ceiling. No lying there doing math about whether it'll make it to spring.

Getting a house to that place takes a few things we decided we'd never compromise on. We'd build roofs for our climate — real underlayment, proper flashing, moss-aware details — not the bare minimum that passes inspection in a dry state. We'd use materials we actually stand behind, which is why we became an Owens Corning contractor. And we'd tell people the truth even when it cost us a sale.

The honest-answer rule

That last one is the part we're most stubborn about. Roof repair is a service we're glad to sell — so when we get on your roof and it's got years of life left, we say so. We'll tell you to wait. We'll show you photos of what's actually up there and give you the real call: maintain it, repair this one spot, or replace it. We would genuinely rather do a small honest repair today and earn the replacement in five years than talk you into something you don't need and lose you as a neighbor forever.

It's why we make our free inspection so easy to say yes to, and why we point people to plain-English resources instead of a sales pitch. If you want to see how we think, read our guide on the signs you actually need a new roof, or the one on how to verify any Oregon contractor in two minutes — and yes, we tell you to run that check on us. A company that's being straight with you has nothing to hide behind a license lookup.

Licensed, insured, and local

Oregon CCB #255729, family-owned, based in Riddle, serving Roseburg and all of Douglas County. Same family answers the phone that shows up on your roof. That's not a slogan — it's just how a small local company works.

More than roofs

Roofing is our heart, but "Construction" is in the name for a reason. Once neighbors trusted us on the roof, they started asking about everything else — the gutters that keep failing, the siding that's tired, a deck, a fence. So we grew into it honestly, one skill at a time, and today we handle a good range of work across the property. You can see the full list on our services page. The through-line never changed: whatever we're building, it's for this climate, and it's built so you don't have to think about it again for a long time.

Where we are now

We've grown, but we've stayed the thing we set out to be — a local family you can call by name. If your roof has been on your mind lately, or you just want a straight answer about how much life it's got left, that's exactly what we're here for. Right now every new roof includes $500 off, plus free whole-home gutters on qualifying roofs ($20k and up), so if it's time, we've tried to make it a good time.

Get a fast ballpark from our instant estimate, or just call or text (541) 670-5005 and talk to us. And if you'd like to meet the people behind the name, our about page is a good next stop. Either way — thanks for reading, neighbor. We hope the next time it rains, you sleep right through it.
