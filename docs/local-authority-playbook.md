## E) Local link-building targets

Local links from Douglas County sites are worth more than generic national links. Pursue these:

**Memberships & associations**
- **Roseburg Area Chamber of Commerce** (https://www.roseburgareachamber.org/) — join; get the member-directory listing (link + citation) and use the member badge on your site.
- **Oregon CCB** (https://www.oregon.gov/ccb/) — ensure the public license record for CCB #255729 is complete and links back where possible.

**Supplier & brand pages**
- **Owens Corning contractor locator** (https://www.owenscorning.com/en-us/roofing/contractors) — brand link + high-intent referral traffic. Ask your Owens Corning rep about being featured or added to any "preferred contractor" listings.
- Local roofing/building-material suppliers you buy from — many list the contractors they serve; ask for a link.

**Local sponsorships (link + goodwill)**
- Sponsor a Douglas County youth sports team, school event, fair, or community fundraiser — most post a sponsor page that links back.
- Local charity / community events in Riddle and Roseburg — donation or in-kind (a repaired roof) often earns a press mention + link.

**Community & regional sites**
- Douglas County community and city sites, "local business" spotlights, and neighborhood directories.
- Local Facebook/Nextdoor community groups (not a backlink, but referral traffic + reputation).

**Local news / press ideas** (pitch = story, not an ad)
- Seasonal storm-prep or "how to spot roof damage before winter" tips offered as a local expert quote to Roseburg-area news.
- The $500-off + free-gutters offer as a community-value angle (limited-time homeowner savings).
- A free/discounted roof for a local family or veteran in need — strong human-interest press hook.
- Milestone stories: family-owned business anniversaries, hiring locally, jobs completed in specific Douglas County towns.

> For every link opportunity, provide them the one-line NAP from Section A and a link to https://mistletoeconstruction.com.

## F) Review-generation SOP — goal 25+ reviews

**Your review link (share this exact URL):**
> https://g.page/r/CYxQiWWU9vkMEBM/review

### The rule: ask EVERY customer, every time
Reviews are the single biggest lever on local rankings and conversion. The businesses with the most recent reviews win the map. Make asking a non-negotiable step of every completed job.

### The ask (say it out loud at job completion)
> "We're a family-owned local business and Google reviews are how folks around Douglas County find us. If you're happy with your new roof, would you mind leaving us a quick review? I'll text you the direct link right now — it takes about 30 seconds."

Then send the link immediately while you're standing there.

### Text/email template (send within 1 hour of finishing)
> Thanks for trusting Mistletoe Construction with your roof! A quick Google review really helps our family business. Here's the direct link (30 seconds): https://g.page/r/CYxQiWWU9vkMEBM/review — Thank you! (541) 670-5005

### Cadence
1. **At completion:** ask in person, send the link on the spot.
2. **+1 day:** if no review, send the text template once.
3. **+4 days:** one friendly follow-up ("no worries if you're busy — link's here whenever: …"). Then stop. Never nag past two follow-ups.
4. **Ongoing:** put the review link in email signatures, invoices, business cards (QR code), and the website footer so the ask never depends on memory.

### Getting to 25+
- Ask every single customer — at ~50% follow-through you clear 25 quickly.
- Also (politely) invite past happy customers you haven't reviewed yet: "We just started focusing on Google reviews — would you mind sharing your experience?"
- Never buy, incentivize, or gate reviews (against Google policy and it can get the profile suspended). Just ask, consistently.

### Handling negative reviews
- **Respond within 24 hours, publicly, calm and professional.** Never argue.
- Template: "Thank you for the feedback, [Name]. We're sorry your experience didn't meet the standard we hold as a family-owned business. Please call or text me directly at (541) 670-5005 so we can make this right." Then take it offline.
- Fix the underlying issue; if resolved, some customers will update their rating.
- A few honest negatives among many positives actually build trust — don't panic. Keep generating new positive reviews so any negative gets outweighed and pushed down.
